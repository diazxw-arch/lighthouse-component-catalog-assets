# LH01-0001 · 音声入力付き検索欄

Reusable controlled React component: `VoiceSearchField`. The application owns
the query, search results, back navigation and voice integration. The preview
executes its callbacks and reports honestly that it has no voice recognizer;
it never requests microphone access or pretends to record. Initial presentation
contains the component alone. Search submit, clear, back and voice are separate.
Japanese IME composition does not accidentally submit. Disabled, searching and
error states have accessible feedback; Escape clears and keyboard focus stays
visible with neutral feedback. Reduced motion disables transitions.

```tsx
<VoiceSearchField value={query} onValueChange={setQuery} onSearch={searchPlaces}
  onBack={goBack} onVoiceRequest={startVoiceInput} busy={searching} error={error} />
```

Target: Image Gen v003 and its correction metadata, with the saved Google Maps
original governing fine icon geometry. The capsule is white, compact and neutral;
Meiryo 14/24, tracking 0, with existing licensed Noto Sans JP subsets as portable
fallback. Google Material icons preserve the solid microphone head/U support/
stem and back chevron anatomy. No tint or extra sample-reader content. Hover/
pressed fills remain within the outer capsule; focus is neutral and visible.

No backend/auth data, external speech service, Maker registration, physical-device
acceptance or certified numerical fidelity is claimed. Per-item render/interaction,
catalog, deployment and mock cleanup evidence is maintained in Owner A's ledger.

Third-party notices: Google Material SVG icons (Apache-2.0) and existing Noto
Sans JP fonts (OFL-1.1); their license/source records remain in the delivered app.
