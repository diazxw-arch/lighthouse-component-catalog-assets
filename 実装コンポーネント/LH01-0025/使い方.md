# LH01-0025 · 価格と設備をまとめた絞り込み

Reusable React component: `BudgetEquipmentPanel`.

Composes the existing controlled BudgetRange, RoomQuantityFilters and AmenityChips into one panel with dividers. Exactly parking/air are exposed; all three room minima are initially disabled. Changes preserve sibling fields. Illustrative histogram frequencies approximate the original shape and are not real market data.

Native Meiryo with existing licensed Noto Sans JP fallback; role sizes follow the source instruction. Source/original/Image Gen histories remain separate from rendered QA and live verification. No pixel identity, physical-device/human acceptance, service integration or Maker registration is claimed.
