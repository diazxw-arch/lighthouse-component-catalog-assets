# LH01-0022 · 横送りの検索条件ツールバー

Reusable React component: `SearchConditionToolbar`.

Horizontal no-wrap scrolling keeps filters, sort, open-now, price and the final takeout condition accessible. Stable controlled booleans remain separate from utility callbacks. Preview sort/price/filter editors change local state after activation; they do not query a backend. The next clipped label is the catalog translation, not recovered hidden original text.

Native Meiryo with existing licensed Noto Sans JP fallback; role sizes follow the source instruction. Source/original/Image Gen histories remain separate from rendered QA and live verification. No pixel identity, physical-device/human acceptance, service integration or Maker registration is claimed.
