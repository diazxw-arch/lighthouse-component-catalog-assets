# LH01-0046 · 都市名と保存操作の地図シート

Reusable React composition: CityMapSheet.

Compact native city sheet with20/28 Bold title,14/24 Regular labels, top expansion affordance and three native actions. Forest-gradient directions retains the diamond/right-turn glyph. Unsaved/saved bookmark is caller-controlled. The label flag is source-specific editable SVG; geometry passed actual four-width comparison.
