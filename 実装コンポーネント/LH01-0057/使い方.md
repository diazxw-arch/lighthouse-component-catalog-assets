# LH01-0057 · 地域と検索条件を二段で示す検索窓

Reusable React component: PlaceSearchSummaryBar.

Three independent 44px native buttons for back, a centered two-line search summary, and filters. The editable left arrow has its original shaft; filter geometry has three horizontal lines and outlined knobs at right-left-right. Titles, summary and real filter expansion are caller-owned. No route or filter panel is fabricated.

The initial preview shows only this component. Honest disconnected-service feedback appears only after an action. Required Noto/source notices remain in an inert delivered template. Original and Image Gen history are preserved. Source checks, actual rendered acceptance, release and native/mock retirement are separate.
